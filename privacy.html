1. Company and scope

This policy describes the known handling of information by InfoSea Automation, a private internal-use application operated by INFOSEA CO., a Washington State profit corporation with the registered trade name INFOSEA CO and a business location in Seattle, Washington, United States. Its Washington State Business License was issued on October 31, 2025.

The Application is not intended for public users or external customers.

2. Information currently handled

Depending on an authorized workflow, the Application may access or process vendor and item information, purchase-order records, original customer purchase-order PDF files, SQLite records, and workflow logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the process.

3. How information is used

Information is used to support authorized InfoSea purchasing operations, including creating, reading, verifying, and sending vendor purchase orders, looking up vendor and item information, associating original purchase-order documentation with workflow records, maintaining business records, and troubleshooting the Application.

4. QuickBooks access

The Application accesses QuickBooks data through Intuit APIs when authorized by InfoSea. Sandbox OAuth has been implemented and tested. Production OAuth endpoints are not yet configured or verified; this policy does not claim that a production connection is currently available.

5. Local storage

The current agent operates locally on a dedicated Mac mini and uses local files, SQLite workflow records, archived PDF files, workflow logs, and locally stored QuickBooks OAuth tokens. The Mac mini is currently used only by the authorized operator responsible for InfoSea Automation.

The QuickBooks OAuth token file is stored locally in the Application’s work directory. It is owned by the dedicated macOS account sasagent and has file permissions set to 600 (-rw-------), which restrict read and write access to that owning account.

The Mac mini uses macOS FileVault full-disk encryption. This is device-level encryption only. The OAuth token file is not represented as separately encrypted or stored in macOS Keychain because those controls have not been verified.

No Apple Account, iCloud Drive, or Time Machine backup is configured on the Mac mini.

6. Sharing and service providers

Intuit processes information as necessary to provide QuickBooks Online and its APIs.

The current Application does not intentionally send purchase-order documents, customer or vendor information, or QuickBooks data to external artificial-intelligence services. This statement is limited to the current Application and does not make a broader representation about every possible form of third-party processing that has not been verified.

7. Security

InfoSea restricts the dedicated Mac mini to its current authorized operator. macOS FileVault full-disk encryption is enabled. Remote Login (SSH) is disabled. Screen Sharing remains enabled solely for the authorized operator’s remote administration.

QuickBooks OAuth tokens are stored locally in a file owned by the sasagent account with permissions set to 600. Separate file-level encryption and macOS Keychain storage have not been verified and are not claimed.

No Apple Account, iCloud Drive, or Time Machine backup is configured. Any additional technical or organizational safeguards will be described only after verification.

8. Retention and deletion

InfoSea currently retains business-related purchase-order PDF files, SQLite records, and workflow logs for ongoing business and recordkeeping purposes. No fixed deletion schedule or automatic deletion policy has been established.

QuickBooks OAuth tokens are retained locally while needed to maintain an authorized connection or support the Application’s operation. Production disconnection and token-revocation procedures have not yet been configured or verified.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated if the Application’s functions, data practices, service providers, or legal requirements change.

10. Contact
