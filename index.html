About the application

InfoSea Automation is intended solely for authorized INFOSEA CO. personnel. The current workflow supports creating, reading, verifying, and sending vendor purchase orders; looking up vendor and item information; and attaching original customer purchase-order PDF files.

Public access, marketplace distribution, and services for external customers are not planned.
